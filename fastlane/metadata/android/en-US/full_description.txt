<p>OpenRath is a free, open source home screen for any Android-based aftermarket car stereo, whatever its make. It replaces the stereo's own launcher with a dashboard you can rearrange.</p>

<p><i>Rath</i> is Sanskrit for chariot.</p>

<p><b>On the dashboard</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Your car with live speed, and the clock</li>
<li>Weather and rain on the way</li>
<li>A map with turn-by-turn guidance, place search and offline map pictures</li>
<li>Radio and music, including online radio</li>
<li>A trip computer and fuel log</li>
<li>Driving helpers: speed limits and cameras, parking spot, service reminders, dashcam, voice commands and steering-wheel buttons</li>
</ul>
<p>Every feature has its own switch, and a Simple, Full or Custom level picks a sensible set to start with.</p>

<p><b>Works with any car</b></p>
<p>On the first start a short setup asks for the brand, model, fuel (petrol, diesel, CNG, hybrid or electric) and tank size. The name beside the logo, the service centre search, fuel words and expected mileage all follow it.</p>

<p><b>Works on any screen</b></p>
<p>The dashboard is designed at 1024 x 600 and scales to fit 7 to 13 inch screens, 1280 x 720 and ultra-wide 1920 x 720. Portrait screens stack the boxes with the apps along the bottom.</p>

<p><b>Works on any Android stereo</b></p>
<p>OpenRath runs on plain Android 8.0 and newer. The radio box follows any radio app's media controls, and calls go through the system phone (Bluetooth hands-free on most units). Optional add-ons for Topway, FYT and MTCD/Joying units add FM in the radio box, Bluetooth calls and reverse gear through the stereo's own services. They load only on that make and only while their switch is on.</p>

<p><b>Privacy</b></p>
<p>No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Trips, logs and settings stay on the unit. Online features (weather, news, map, routes, place search, online radio) talk directly to the services they use, and each can be switched off.</p>

<p><b>Install</b></p>
<p>After installing, press Home and pick OpenRath, then Always. The stereo's own launcher is never changed or removed, and you can go back to it from Android's Default apps settings.</p>
