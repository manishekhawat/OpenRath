OPENRATH THEME
================

This folder is a theme. Change what you like, then in OpenRath open
Settings > Dashboard > Theme pack and pick this folder's name.
Tap it again after editing to reload your changes.

theme.json
  colours       Colours as #RRGGBB (or #AARRGGBB for see-through).
                text        main text and numbers
                textDim     smaller, quieter text
                accent      labels such as "LIGHTS ON" and highlights
                edge        the thin border round each box
                panelTop    box colour at the top (it fades to "panel")
                panel       box colour at the bottom
                background  behind everything
  cornerRadius  how round the box corners are (0 = square, 14 = default)
  road          true or false: the moving road under the car
  font          a .otf or .ttf file in this folder for the welcome line
  welcomeTitle  the word above the name (default WELCOME)
  carName       the name beside the logo (default: the car's model; the owner can change it)

Pictures (PNG, keep the same names; anything left out uses the built-in one)
  car_off.png, car_on.png       the car with headlights off / on
  logo.png                      the logo top-left in the car box (the car name is text)
  dock_navi.png … dock_settings.png   the buttons down the left
  btn_play.png, btn_pause.png, btn_prev.png, btn_next.png
  w_sun.png, w_moon.png, w_partly.png, w_cloud.png, w_rain.png,
  w_snow.png, w_storm.png, w_fog.png   weather icons
  background.jpg or background.png     optional wallpaper (1024x600 or 1280x720)

Pictures are drawn for a 600-pixel-tall screen at 2.4x; keep similar sizes
and transparent backgrounds.

SHARING
  Zip this folder and share the .zip. Others put it in Download and use
  Settings > Dashboard > Import a theme from Download.
